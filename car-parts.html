Car Parts

Free 3D print files

Files are free from their creators. We charge for the printing.

Old-school American cars break the little plastic bits first — the clips, bezels, vents, and mounts nobody makes anymore. We print them. Adapters, mounts, brackets, emblems, gauge pods, cup holders, speaker adapters, and wire looms — if it’s plastic and it fits your car, we can probably make it. Don’t see your car on this page? Tell us what you drive and what you need — we’ll research it and track the part down for you.

Best bets to start with: the dash bezel for GloShift gauges, the 6×9 rear-deck speaker adapter, the spark plug wire loom kit, and the El Camino bed molding clips. Most small parts start at our $15 minimum — get an instant quote with your file, or text us for a price. Bring your own model file, or just tell us what broke and we’ll design it.

Printing is $0.25/g PLA ($0.30/g silk), $15 minimum — the quote tool gives you the exact price. The designer is credited on each listing, and we encourage you to support the creators directly (memberships, tips, follows).

Can’t find your part?

Don’t see your vehicle here? No problem — tell us what you drive and what you need. We’ll research it, find the part or the file, and print it for you. Send a photo, the year and model, or your own STL file — we’ll design it, print it, and hold it for pickup at 1717 O St, deliver it in town, or mail it to you. We’re also working on the hard ones nobody makes anymore, like the wagon hatch clips for the 80s GM wagons and power mirror frames. Bigger custom projects — like full door panels with speaker pods — are possible too; just ask.

Cabin parts are printed in ASA or PETG, not PLA — they hold up in a hot car. Engine-bay parts get ASA or nylon. We don’t print brakes, steering, fuel-system, wheel, or structural parts.
