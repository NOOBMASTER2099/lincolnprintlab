Terms & disclosure

Plain English, no fine-print games. Please read this before you pay — by placing an order, you agree to these terms.

All sales are final

Every print is made to order just for you, so we don’t take returns or exchanges because you changed your mind, picked the wrong color or size, or decided you don’t want it after all. This policy is shown to you here, before you pay. It does not cover a print that arrives broken, defective, or different from what you ordered — see “If something’s wrong” below.

If we can’t make it, you get your money back

If your print fails, we can’t accept the order, or we can’t get it to you by the estimated date, we’ll text you and you can cancel for a full refund. Refunds go back the same way you paid (Cash App, Zelle, or PayPal) within 3–5 business days. If we miss the estimated date, we’ll tell you before that date passes and give you a new date or a full refund — your choice. This follows the FTC’s Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule: if a seller can’t ship on time, you get the choice to wait or cancel for a prompt refund, and the refund can’t be store credit.

If something’s wrong with your print

“All sales final” doesn’t cover a print that arrives broken, defective, or different from what you ordered. If that happens, text us within a reasonable time after you get it and we’ll make it right — reprint it, replace it, or refund you. A product has to be fit for its ordinary purpose, and no return policy overrides that.

What you pay for

Prices on the site are estimates. Matt confirms the exact total by text before you pay — you don’t pay until he confirms. You pay shipping and handling: mailed orders are at cost (about $6 for small items). Delivery in Lincoln has a fee, charged at checkout and confirmed by text. Pickup at 1717 O St is free. Nebraska sales tax applies — 5.5% state plus local, based on where it’s delivered (Lincoln is 7.25% total). Rush (1–2 business days) is +50%.

Safety — please read this

Cosplay helmets and props are costume pieces only. They are not protective gear. Do not wear them on a scooter, motorcycle, bike, or anywhere you need real protection — they will not protect you in a crash. PLA prints are not food-safe — don’t use them for food or drink, because the layer lines can harbor bacteria. Car parts are for non-safety-critical use only (brackets, mounts, clips, bezels, cup holders) — never brakes, steering, or anything that keeps you safe on the road.

Character designs & your files

Some designs are characters owned by Marvel, DC, and other studios. Where a design is marked “Display only,” it’s for personal use and display — we don’t sell prints of it as merchandise. If you send us your own file, you’re responsible for it: you have the right to print it, and it doesn’t break anyone’s copyright or trademark. If we can’t print something, we’ll tell you before you pay.

Your order & your info

We use your name, phone, and email only to make and deliver your order and to text you about it. We don’t sell your info. Payment happens in your Cash App, Zelle, or PayPal app — we never see or store your card number. Because there’s no card form on this site, there’s nothing for anyone to steal here.

Estimates, not guarantees

Print times, prices, and delivery dates are estimates. The exact price and date are confirmed by text before we print. If anything changes — a print fails, a part takes longer, the queue is full — we’ll text you first, and you can cancel for a full refund if the new date doesn’t work for you.

Questions or a problem?

Text (402) 413-5332 or email contact@lincolnprintlab.com. If we can’t sort it out together, you can also contact the Nebraska Attorney General’s consumer protection office or the FTC.
