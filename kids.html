Kids Stuff

Free 3D print files

Files are free from their creators. We charge for the printing.

Pick from our inventory — toy cars, animals, dinosaurs, puzzles, piggy banks and more for kids. Download the model you like, then send us the file and we'll give you an exact quote. Everything here is kid-friendly — no safety-critical parts, and anything with small pieces is flagged. Don't see what you need? Tell us what you're looking for — we'll research it and track it down for you.

Printing is $0.25/g PLA ($0.30/g silk), $15 minimum — the quote tool gives you the exact price. The designer is credited on each listing, and we encourage you to support the creators directly (memberships, tips, follows).

A note on kids’ items: everything here is kid-friendly — no safety-critical parts, and anything with small pieces is flagged on its card. PLA isn’t food-safe, so cookie cutters are for dough only and bath toys are for water play, not chewing. Items meant for kids under 12 follow CPSIA guidance — ask us if you need a specific material or finish.
