Materials

A quick guide to what we print. Not sure? Start with PLA — it’s the easy everyday choice — and text us if you want a recommendation.

PLA / PLA+ / Silk / Matte

Easy, bright, and beginner-friendly. Great for prototypes, gifts, display pieces, and everyday prints. Silk looks shiny; matte looks soft.

PETG

Tougher and a bit more flexible than PLA. Good for functional parts, brackets, cases, and stuff that gets handled a lot.

TPU

Rubbery and bendy. Phone bumpers, grips, gaskets, flexible hinges — anything that needs to flex without snapping.

ABS

Strong and heat-resistant. Solid pick for tougher functional parts that see warmth or wear.

ASA

Like ABS, but better outdoors. UV-resistant — good for garden, vehicle, and sun-facing parts.

Nylon

Strong and durable for demanding parts. Needs dry filament for best results — tell us if that’s the goal.
