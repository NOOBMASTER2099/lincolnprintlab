Cosplay Helmets & Props

Free 3D print files

Files are free from their creators. We charge for the printing.

Pick from our inventory — helmets, masks, armor, and props to wear to the con or hang on the wall. Download the model you like, then send us the file and we’ll give you an exact quote. Most of these are character pieces (Marvel, Star Wars, games), so they’re display-only at the shop — but we can still print one for your own costume. Don’t see what you need? Tell us what you’re looking for — we’ll research it and track it down for you.

Printing is $0.25/g PLA ($0.30/g silk), $15 minimum — the quote tool gives you the exact price. The designer is credited on each listing, and we encourage you to support the creators directly (memberships, tips, follows).

Not sure of your size? Check the Helmet Sizing guide — the LincolnPrintLab Scale matches your hat size to the right print size.
